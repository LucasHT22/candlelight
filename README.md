# Candlelight: the light within you

## Our prompt

> A distant light may arrive exactly when it is needed

## Storytelling

You are a little candle, lost in the dark.

It’s midnight, and the world outside is filled with monsters, deep pits, and dangers hiding just beyond the reach of your flame.

You just want to get home.

But there’s a problem:

Your light is your life.

As you explore, you’ll have to use your own flame to light the way, solve puzzles, activate mechanisms, and find paths through the darkness.

Sometimes, getting closer to home means making a sacrifice.

Burn part of yourself to light a path.

Give up some of your flame to activate something.

Risk becoming smaller and weaker just to keep moving forward.

The game combines 2D platforming, parkour, exploration, and environmental puzzles, with the candle’s light being the main mechanic.

The darkness isn’t just something you have to survive.

It’s the world you have to learn to use.

And somewhere beyond all that darkness…
your people are waiting for you. 

The light you need to find your way home is always with you — but to use it, you have to sacrifice a little of yourself.
